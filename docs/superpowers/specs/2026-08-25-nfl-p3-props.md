# NFL P3 — Player props (game-script volume × efficiency, injury-aware)

**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Status:** Approved design (pre-plan)
**Parent spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-23-nfl-model.md` (P3 phase)
**Depends on:** P2 (merged, commit 6f875a5) — `nfl/gameline.py` (implied team totals + spread per
game), committed `assets/nfl/*.parquet` (weekly/rosters/injuries), and P1's `nfl/espn.py`
(game-day inactives).

## Problem & goal

NFL player props are the softest market and the primary +EV opportunity (game lines are sharp,
per P2). P3 builds the **projection engine**: turn P2's game line (implied team totals + spread)
into team play volume and pass/run split, allocate that to players by usage share, apply
per-player efficiency, and emit a **calibrated probability distribution per prop market** — the
`prop_predictions.dist` JSONB the existing board/grader already evaluate at the book's line. This
is what gives Week-1 signal with no 2026 data: last-season player rates × depth-chart role,
game-script-adjusted by the current line.

Seven markets (settled): `pass_yds`, `pass_tds`, `reception_yds`, `receptions`, `rush_yds`,
`rush_reception_yds`, `anytime_td`.

## Scope / non-goals

**In (P3):**
- Game-script team-volume model (fitted on 2015–2024): plays + pass rate ~ f(spread, implied total).
- Usage shares (target/carry/pass-attempt share, shrunk to depth-chart baselines).
- Efficiency (yards/att, catch rate, yards/rec, yards/carry, TD rates, shrunk to position baselines).
- Per-market distributions → P(over)-ready `dist`: yardage Normal, receptions Negative Binomial,
  pass TDs Poisson, anytime TD `1 − Poisson(0)`.
- Player universe from depth charts + injury/inactive filtering + backup bump + book-name matching.
- Walk-forward backtest + per-market calibration (distributions calibrated to actual outcomes).
- Acquire the most recent completed season's weekly stats (2025 if available; see Data).

**Out (P4+):**
- The `generate_nfl` producer wiring props into `prop_predictions`, the NFL board/grade passes,
  and front-end — P3 produces the engine + calibrated distributions; P4 wires them in.
- Kicking/defense/longest-completion/1st-last-TD/INT markets (beyond the seven).
- Any change to the MLB pipeline. NFL is additive.

## Game-script / team-volume model (`nfl/gamescript.py`)

Fit on 2015–2024 team-game rows (aggregate weekly to team-game: `team_pass_att = Σ attempts`,
`team_rush_att = Σ carries`, `team_plays = team_pass_att + team_rush_att`), joined to
`schedules` for each team's line:

- Per team-game, derive the team's own line from the home-perspective `spread_line`/`total_line`:
  `implied_team_total = (total_line + team_margin) / 2`, where `team_margin = spread_line` for the
  home team and `−spread_line` for the away team (convention confirmed in P2: `spread_line`
  positive = home favored).
- Fit two regressions:
  - `pass_rate = team_pass_att / team_plays ~ f(team_margin, implied_team_total)` — trailing/underdog
    teams (negative `team_margin`) pass more; higher implied total shifts the mix.
  - `team_plays ~ g(team_margin, implied_team_total)` — pace/blowout effects (leaders in blowouts
    run fewer plays; higher totals slightly more).
  Linear (optionally with a quadratic `team_margin` term); fit + validated like P1/P2 (train/holdout).
- **Predict:** consume P2's `gameline` output directly — `pred_home_score`/`pred_away_score` ARE the
  implied team totals and `pred_margin` is the home team margin. For each team: `team_plays`,
  `pass_rate` from the regressions → `team_pass_att`, `team_rush_att`.
- **API:** `fit_gamescript(team_games) -> GameScriptModel`; `project_team_volume(model, team_margin,
  implied_team_total) -> {pass_att, rush_att, plays}`.

## Usage shares (`nfl/usage.py`)

From the most recent completed season, per player on their team:
- `target_share = player_targets / team_targets`, `carry_share = player_carries / team_carries`,
  `pass_att_share = player_attempts / team_attempts` (starting QB ≈ 1.0).
- **Shrink** each share toward a depth-chart/position baseline by an empirical-Bayes factor
  `n_games / (n_games + K_usage)` (mirrors MLB's `shrink_toward_league` and P1/P2's early shrinkage).
- **Allocate** team volume → per-player: `player_targets = team_pass_att × target_share`,
  `player_carries = team_rush_att × carry_share`, `qb_pass_att = team_pass_att × pass_att_share`.
- **API:** `compute_usage_shares(weekly_last_season, k_usage) -> {player_id: {target_share, carry_share,
  pass_att_share, position, team}}`.

## Efficiency (`nfl/efficiency.py`)

Per player, most recent completed season, shrunk to position baselines (empirical-Bayes):
- `ypa = passing_yards/attempts`, `pass_td_rate = passing_tds/attempts`.
- `catch_rate = receptions/targets`, `ypr = receiving_yards/receptions`, `rec_td_rate = receiving_tds/targets`.
- `ypc = rushing_yards/carries`, `rush_td_rate = rushing_tds/carries`.
- **API:** `compute_efficiency(weekly_last_season, k_eff) -> {player_id: {ypa, pass_td_rate, catch_rate,
  ypr, rec_td_rate, ypc, rush_td_rate}}`.

## Per-market distributions (`nfl/props.py`, reusing `model/distributions.py`)

For a player, combine usage-allocated volume × efficiency into a projected mean, then a distribution
whose `dist` JSONB is evaluated at the book line by the existing `prob_over_dist`:
- `pass_yds` mean `= qb_pass_att × ypa` → **Normal**(mean, σ_pass_yds) → `{"kind":"normal","mean","sd"}`.
- `reception_yds` mean `= (targets × catch_rate) × ypr` → **Normal**(mean, σ_rec_yds).
- `rush_yds` mean `= carries × ypc` → **Normal**(mean, σ_rush_yds).
- `rush_reception_yds` mean `= rush_yds_mean + rec_yds_mean` → **Normal**(mean, σ_rush_rec) (σ fit jointly).
- `receptions` mean `= targets × catch_rate` → **Negative Binomial** via `distributions.nb_pmf(mean, var)`
  → `{"kind":"pmf","pmf":[...]}`.
- `pass_tds` mean `= qb_pass_att × pass_td_rate` → **Poisson**(mean) → `{"kind":"pmf","pmf":[...]}`.
- `anytime_td`: `λ = carries × rush_td_rate + targets × rec_td_rate`; `P(≥1) = 1 − e^{−λ}` → store the
  Poisson pmf `{"kind":"pmf","pmf":[...]}` so `prob_over_dist(dist, 0.5)` = P(≥1). (Board treats it as
  the over on 0.5, like MLB's HR Y/N.)
- **API:** `build_prop(market, volume, efficiency, cfg) -> dict` returning `{projected_mean, dist}`.

## Player universe + injuries (`nfl/universe.py`)

- **Universe:** projected skill players per team from `rosters.depth_chart_position` (QB/RB/HB/FB/WR/TE),
  top of each depth chart, for the upcoming week.
- **Injury/inactive filter (required):** exclude players with `injuries.report_status == "Out"` (join by
  `gsis_id`) AND ESPN game-day inactives (P1 `espn.fetch_inactives`, the more current source). **Never
  post a prop for an inactive player.**
- **Backup bump:** when a starter is Out, redistribute their usage share to the next depth-chart player at
  the position.
- **Name matching:** book `player_name` → our `player_id` by normalized-name match (same approach as MLB
  props), scoped to the game's two teams.
- **API:** `active_universe(rosters, injuries, espn_inactives, week) -> list[player]`;
  `match_book_player(name, universe) -> player_id | None`.

## Backtest + calibration (`scripts/backtest_nfl_props.py`)

Walk-forward on weekly (2015–2024; +2025 if acquired): for each player-game, project from PRIOR-season
usage+efficiency and the game's actual team line (via `gamescript`) → compare the projected distribution
to the actual stat.
- **Fit** per-market σ (yardage), NB dispersion (receptions), and a **calibration layer** — Platt on
  P(over) at reference lines + loc/scale on the yardage means — so the distributions are well-calibrated to
  reality (mirrors the MLB props calibration).
- **No historical prop lines exist** (The Odds API is capture-forward; nflverse has no prop lines), so
  calibrate the distributions to ACTUAL OUTCOMES (coverage / PIT / reliability), not to a market. State this
  honestly. TD markets calibrated but expected loose (low-count, high-variance — the P1/P2 honesty lesson).
- **Output:** committed `assets/nfl/props.json` (per-market σ's, NB dispersion, calibration params,
  K_usage/K_eff) + a findings report under `docs/superpowers/reports/`.

## Acceptance / done bar (P3)

- Game-script regressions fit and validate OOS; `project_team_volume` gives sane plays/pass-att across the
  spread/total range (e.g. a big underdog projects more pass attempts than a big favorite).
- Usage shares sum sanely per team (target/carry shares ≈ 1.0 across a team's players) and shrink toward
  baselines for low-sample players; efficiency shrinks to position baselines.
- Each market's `build_prop` emits a valid `dist` (pmf sums ≈ 1 or a normal), consumable by
  `prob_over_dist` unchanged; `anytime_td` `prob_over_dist(dist, 0.5)` = `1 − e^{−λ}`.
- Universe filtering never yields an inactive player; a starter-Out case bumps the backup.
- Backtest: per-market distributions calibrated to outcomes (reliability reasonable on yardage/receptions;
  TD loose but not broken); reported with statistical-honesty framing.
- **No MLB regression** — full suite green; NFL additive.

## Testing approach (TDD)

- `gamescript.py`: fitted model gives higher pass_att for a big underdog than a big favorite; plays sane;
  deterministic.
- `usage.py`: shares computed correctly; shrinkage pulls a low-games player toward baseline; allocation
  multiplies team volume by share.
- `efficiency.py`: rates computed; shrinkage to position baseline.
- `props.py`: each market's dist is valid (Normal keys / pmf sums≈1); `receptions` NB mean matches input;
  `pass_tds` Poisson mean matches; `anytime_td` P(≥1) = `1 − e^{−λ}` via `prob_over_dist(dist, 0.5)`;
  yardage mean = volume × efficiency.
- `universe.py`: OUT player excluded; ESPN inactive excluded; starter-out bumps backup; name match +
  no-match → None.
- Backtest: runs on a small slice → per-market metrics dict; calibration params returned; determinism; a
  leak-regression check (project from PRIOR seasons only).
- Full MLB suite stays green.

## Build ordering (for the plan)

Yardage-first: (1) data/2025 acquisition, (2) game-script model, (3) usage, (4) efficiency, (5) yardage +
receptions distributions, (6) universe/injuries + name matching, (7) backtest/calibration for the yardage
markets — then (8) the TD/anytime distributions (Poisson) + their calibration as a thin final task. If the
runway tightens, yardage props are shippable after task 7.

## Risks

- **Week-1 cold start** — no 2026 data; last-season rates + depth-chart role + game-script carry it; rookies/
  new roles fall back to position baselines. The market shrinkage on the game line already tempers the
  volume inputs.
- **2025 data availability** — see Data; degrade to 2024 + refresh-fold-in, pipeline prefers newest.
- **No historical prop market** — calibrate to outcomes, not market; CLV is the season-long judge (Week-1 is
  the launch, not the proof).
- **TD/longshot noise** — pass-TD and anytime-TD are low-count/binary, high-variance; looser calibration than
  yardage (built last, expected loose).
- **Injury/inactive lag** — dual source (nflverse `report_status` + ESPN game-day actives); ESPN is the
  final gate on game day.
- **Usage volatility** — target/carry shares shift with scheme/injuries; empirical-Bayes shrinkage + the
  backup-bump handle the obvious cases; mid-season role changes are a known residual.
